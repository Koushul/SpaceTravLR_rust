4. Takeaways

  • SpaceTravLR β leads on unsupervised spatial consistency (CAS 0.90, MLAMI 0.85, GCS 0.95).
  • BANKSY has the highest CNMI (0.23) but weaker spatial conservation — and slightly higher (still low) zone ARI, consistent with expression-driven niches.
  • COVET is the closest method to SpaceTravLR β by pairwise ARI; NicheCompass niches are largely orthogonal to the other three.
  • Do not score methods by GC zone ARI — those labels come from expression.
